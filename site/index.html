how it works

One read, step by step

No install, no wallet, no key on your side. The desk talks to public RPC, Dexscreener, PumpPortal and xAI. That is the whole list.

01 · curve

The bonding curve

The pump.fun curve account for the mint: who created it, how far along it is, whether it graduated to a pool. One RPC read.

02 · holders

Top holders, named

The twenty largest token accounts, each resolved to its owner and its owning program, so the curve and the pools are excluded and the top-10 share is people, not plumbing.

03 · creator

The creator wallet

Up to a thousand signatures: first seen, last seen, how busy. The SOL balance. How much of the supply it still holds. A wallet born this morning is a line on the card.

04 · market

Dexscreener

Market cap, liquidity, 24h volume, buys and sells, the listing's website and social links, paid boosts. The X handle on the listing is handed to Grok.

05 · grok on x

Grok reads X

xAI's Responses API with the x_search tool, last seven days, a few searches: ticker, name, mint, the handle. It answers in json: posts, accounts, account ages, repeated text, the project's own account, big accounts, five quotes.

06 · card

The card

Lines in three colours: info, note, alert. An alert is a count past a published threshold, nothing more. Time and cost at the bottom. Then it is on the wall.

the token

$LEVEL pays the desk's bill

The desk reads with a real Grok key and every read costs real money. The project token is how that bill gets paid and how the desk stays free for anyone who pastes a mint. The token does nothing inside the desk: it never gates a read, never changes a line.

mintposted on the project account on launch day

what the site will never do

The buy button opens Jupiter's swap in your own wallet; the site holds nothing and signs nothing. The desk has no wallet and no key to one. No part of the code can send a transaction: the RPC client refuses any method that is not a read before the request is built.

hard limits

Never

×
Never a price call

No score, no rating, no target, no "safe", no "rug". Counts past a published threshold, and quotes from real posts. The person decides.

×
Never a signer

No private key anywhere. The RPC client has an allow-list of read methods and refuses everything else before a request exists.

×
Never your data

A read is not logged with who asked. No IP, no address, no timestamp of the request. The card goes on the wall without you.

×
Never an invented post

Grok is told to never invent a post. Every quote carries its URL. If it found nothing, the card says it found nothing.

×
Never pretends the budget is there

When the day's Grok budget is spent, the card says so and shows chain facts only. No silent degrade.

×
Never gated by the token

Holding $LEVEL changes nothing on this site. The token pays the bill, it does not buy a better card.
